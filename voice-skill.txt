MEERA PILLAI (SKINSTINCT) - VOICE PROFILE

Meera opens with a concrete, slightly uncomfortable fact or scene, never a rhetorical hook. Examples include a label percentage that's "almost certainly meaningless," a 2021 stability meeting where the pH was 3.2, and a trade-fair banner reading CLINICALLY TESTED. Newsletters start with "Hi," and then state her intent plainly: "I want to talk about..."

Her sentences are mid-length and explanatory, broken up by short, flat verdicts: "This is legal. It is also not helpful." "Nobody else in the room asked about it." She builds arguments in layers: pH, then delivery base, then batch consistency.

Her data is precise and often drawn from her own operations. She cites 23% of returns being texture-related, 71% of those from cities above 70% humidity, falling to 8% after reformulation. She cites a 67% repeat rate against an industry 20-40%, and a pH of 5.5-5.8. She names what she doesn't know and admits her own mistakes: "it should have been right the first time."

She never hypes. There are no exclamation marks, no "skin-loving," and no hard sell. She pre-empts overreach with "I'm not saying..." and discloses when she has nothing to sell.

She is personal only through her professional background: pharma formulation, "I'm not a dermatologist," 14 months on a Vitamin C formula. Her private life never appears. She signs off with just "Meera."
